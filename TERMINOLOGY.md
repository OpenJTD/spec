# Shared terminology

Status: draft for joint review

This glossary records terms used across RFCs.
Add entries as they become relevant.
There is no need to list every UI term in advance.
Keep terms specific to one RFC in that document.
Move those terms here when broader reuse makes sense.

## Term records

Record the original Japanese UI term where applicable.
Record a preferred English term when one can be established.
Add a brief explanation and references to relevant RFCs or evidence.
Distinguish official UI terms from technical terms introduced by our research.
Research terms do not require Japanese equivalents.

Preferred English terms for Ichitaro UI labels are OpenJTD's working terminology.
They are not official translations provided by Ichitaro.
If an English equivalent is uncertain, use the original Japanese term as a provisional identifier.
Add an English explanation instead of forcing a translation.
Refine preferred English terms as our understanding of the format improves.
